Privacy

This page summarizes how the current extension and this website handle data. The full extension policy is available in English and Korean.

Updated 8 October 2026

On-device processing

Supported-page URLs and limited DOM geometry are processed locally for detection and UI placement. The current general build does not send browsing URLs to the production link API.

Storage and control

Settings, language, comparison selections and public affiliate IDs stay in extension local storage. Session or background-memory records support temporary processing. Disable recommendations, restrict site access or uninstall to remove extension storage.

External destinations

Clicking a configured affiliate link can send the public affiliate ID and destination to the merchant or network. Their policies apply. The extension does not request passwords or payment details, collect browsing-history databases, or include analytics SDKs.

This website

No analytics, advertising trackers or contact forms. GitHub Pages may process request metadata to serve the site. A selected language may be saved in this browser. Email opens your mail app; sending an email shares its contents with RushLabs.
