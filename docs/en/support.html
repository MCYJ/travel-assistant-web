Support

Discover relevant travel, shopping and software services while browsing. pikvia recognizes supported sites and suggests up to three alternatives. Live lowest prices, discounts and purchase attribution are not guaranteed.

Getting started and settings

Open the welcome guide after installation and choose your preferences. Use the pikvia toolbar popup to control the extension, suggestions and language. The extension runs only on supported domains with site access. There is no public store installation link yet.

How affiliate links work

Regular official links and configured affiliate links are identified in the interface. Purchases through affiliate links may earn commission for the configured account owner. Recognizing a site does not imply a partnership, discount or successful purchase attribution.

Local automatic-link scope

In the local testing build, consent and an enabled automatic-partner setting allow eligible homepages to redirect through an issued link. Only verified product routes on selected services preserve product destinations. Other product, search, account, cart, checkout and existing-attribution URLs are excluded. Duplicate checks last 30 minutes. Store builds do not execute automatic visit redirects.

Data and control

Preferences stay in extension storage on your device. Link lookups send service identifiers and scope to our Cloudflare Worker. Supported local product redirects also process a verified public product URL transiently. Homepage lookups do not send the current URL. See the full privacy policy for retention, deletion and third parties.

Troubleshooting

Check extension enablement, site access and suggestion settings. Local automatic links also require separate consent and the enabled setting. Opening a link and verifying cookie or purchase attribution are separate steps. Contact us with browser, version, service name and symptoms; do not send passwords, cookies or complete affiliate URLs.

Disable, remove or contact

Turn features off in the popup, restrict site access in your browser, or uninstall the extension to delete its local storage. Contact RushLabs at june1012june@gmail.com.
